The main divine figures in Kantara are the guardian spirits, or Daivas, Panjurli (a boar spirit), representing nature's protection, and Guliga (a fierce, justice-enforcing spirit), who are central to the film's Bhoota Kola traditions from coastal Karnataka. Panjurli is often linked to Vishnu's Varaha avatar and Parvati, while Guliga is a powerful, sometimes violent, deity representing divine justice, distinct from mainstream Vedic gods but deeply embedded in local folklore
